# Say you are interested

## Overview

One form on the door where a visitor says who they are, how they would like to contribute (funding, design partner, advisor or other) and why. It is stored with the site it came from, Gary is told by email, and a flood of submissions cannot fill the inbox or the table. A submission claims no one's identity: typing an address into the form proves nothing about it. It is planned, not built, and not available.
