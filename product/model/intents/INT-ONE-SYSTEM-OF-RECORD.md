# One system holds everyone who has shown interest or been let in

## Rationale

Interest, invitations, sign-ins and access grants are one history of one person, not four lists in four tools. Holding them in one place, with a person as the thing everything points at, is also what lets a CRM, licence keys and billing join later without a rewrite. Nothing is kept without a stated period, and what is kept about someone can be shown and erased.
