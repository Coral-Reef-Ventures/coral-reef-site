# Streamlane's roadmap

{.lead}
What the Streamlane team has committed to build and when, and the ideas it is weighing next. Streamlane is open to invited guests; its roadmap is open to everyone.

{.note}
It is read from Streamlane's own planning team, which is public, each time this site is built. As on the product's own roadmap, small ideas the team has not put on it are left out, and so are ideas still in its inbox, delivered, parked or declined.
